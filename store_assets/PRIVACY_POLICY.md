# Privacy Policy for HexRush

**Effective Date:** August 25, 2026  
**Last Updated:** August 25, 2026  

HexRush ("we", "our", or "us") is developed as an offline-first single-player tactical hexagonal idle conquest game. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our application handles user information.

---

### 1. Information Collection and Use
HexRush is designed to respect player privacy:
- **No Personal Data Collected:** We do not require account creation, nor do we collect, store, transmit, or share any personal identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, phone number, location, or contacts.
- **Local Game State Storage:** All game progress, high scores, unlocked titles, upgrades, and settings are stored locally on your device using on-device storage (`SharedPreferences` / `UserDefaults`). This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to external servers.

---

### 2. Permissions Used
HexRush requests only the minimum necessary system permissions to function:
- **No Sensitive Permissions:** HexRush does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, location, or photo library.
- **Audio Output:** Used purely for playing localized in-game sound effects and background ambient tracks.

---

### 3. Third-Party Services & Analytics
HexRush does not integrate third-party advertising trackers or invasive analytics SDKs. No tracking identifiers (such as IDFA or GAID) are gathered or shared with data brokers.

---

### 4. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)
HexRush is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under the applicable age in your jurisdiction). Because no personal information is collected, the game complies fully with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and GDPR-K regulations.

---

### 5. Data Retention and Deletion
Since all game data is stored exclusively on your local device:
- You can reset or delete your game data at any time by clearing the application data in your device settings or by uninstalling the application.

---

### 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy.
