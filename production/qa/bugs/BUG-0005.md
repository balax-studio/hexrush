## Reproduction Steps
1. Inspect `lib/domain/economy/economy_calculator.dart` lines 149 to 208.
2. Note that `required Map<String, int> biomeCounts` is passed into `getExpansionCost`.
3. Check where `biomeCounts` is referenced in the method body.

**Expected Result**: Either `biomeCounts` determines per-biome cost scaling (e.g. 5th forest costing more than 1st forest) or the unused parameter is removed/integrated.
**Actual Result**: `biomeCounts` is unused inside `getExpansionCost`; the formula strictly scales exponentially on `ownedCount`.

## Technical Context
- **Likely affected files**:
  - `lib/domain/economy/economy_calculator.dart:152`

## Recommended Fix
Incorporate per-biome scaling into the base cost:
`final int biomeCount = biomeCounts[biome.name] ?? 0;`
`final double biomeMultiplier = math.pow(1.15, biomeCount).toDouble();`
or remove the unused parameter to keep signatures clean.
